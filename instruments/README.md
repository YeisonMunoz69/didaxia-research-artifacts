# Questionnaire items used in the analysis

The files in this directory document Spanish questionnaire wording without releasing responses. They distinguish the recovered closing instruments from earlier paper activities and from the teacher materials prepared before review.

## Student closing questionnaires

[student-closing-items-es.json](student-closing-items-es.json) preserves seven recovered items in revision 6 of the pre-activity form and fourteen in revision 5 of the post-activity form. The export includes item identifiers, Spanish wording, help text, option order, response type, and required or exclusive options where recorded. A blank open-response item appears as a prompt; there are no respondent comments. These files document the recovered revisions rather than every form used in earlier weeks. In the data tables, identifiers such as `F03.1` denote the same item as `F03-1` in the recovered schema.

Items P07 and F08 ask the same question about self-perceived ability to explain the derivative at a point. Their numeric responses support the ordinal comparison; reserved categories remain outside it. The F03 items ask about distinct aspects of experience. F03.5 records overall satisfaction.

## Teacher review

[teacher-rated-items-es.json](teacher-rated-items-es.json) preserves the wording recovered from the applied result exports for four functionality criteria and 26 criteria on mathematical content and sources. The functionality scale lists categories 1–5, `NA` (not applicable), and `PNR` (prefer not to answer); the exported instructions do not supply a verbal anchor for each number. The content scale uses 1 (strongly disagree), 2 (disagree), 3 (neither agree nor disagree), 4 (agree), and 5 (strongly agree), plus `NA` and `II` (insufficient information).

This is an item inventory, rather than a reconstruction of either complete administered form. It excludes profile fields, open questions not needed to interpret the released ratings, individual answers, and attachments. The prepared content package differs at P09: it refers to a specific power, whereas the applied export reads “xn”. The visual attachments used at administration were not recovered in full. The source review concerns two selected Purcell passages and the four criteria P25–P28; it does not cover the entire retrieval corpus.
